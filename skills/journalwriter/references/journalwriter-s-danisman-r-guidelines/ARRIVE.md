# ARRIVE — Experimental Animal Studies

ARRIVE 2.0. The "Essential 10" (mandatory core) + recommended set. The item names are aligned with the official
ARRIVE 2.0.

> Distilled from memory, not from the official source. Verify with the official EQUATOR ARRIVE 2.0
> checklist (arriveguidelines.org / equator-network.org) before submission.

## Essential 10 — mandatory core

1. **Study design:** for each experiment, the experimental and control groups; the number of independent
   experimental units (subjects) in each group.
2. **Sample size:** (a) the exact number of subjects in each group; (b) how the size was determined (give a power
   analysis if any).
3. **Inclusion/exclusion criteria:** (a) pre-specified criteria; (b) subjects/data excluded during the
   analysis and the reason; (c) the analysis stage at which they were applied.
4. **Randomization:** (a) the randomization method used in group assignment; (b) the measure taken to reduce
   confounding effects (cage location, processing order).
5. **Blinding:** at which stage (assignment, processing, outcome assessment) the researchers were blind to
   the group information.
6. **Outcome measures:** the clear definition of the primary and secondary outcomes; how they were measured.
7. **Statistical methods:** (a) detail for each analysis; (b) assessment of the analysis assumptions at the
   experimental-unit level.
8. **Experimental animals:** species, strain, sex, age/weight, health/immune status, genetic
   modification, source.
9. **Experimental procedures:** for each procedure, what, how, when, where, why — reproducible
   detail (including anesthesia, analgesia, doses).
10. **Results:** for each analysis, the effect size estimate and its **precision (95% CI)**; the number of
    subjects per group.

## Recommended additional set

- **Abstract, background, objectives** — context and hypothesis.
- **Ethical approval:** the institution/committee name, the approval number, and the relevant legal framework.
- **Housing and husbandry:** cage, environment, feed, enrichment.
- **Adverse events** and deaths throughout the study.
- **3R implementation** (Replacement, Reduction, Refinement) and improvements.
- **Generalizability / translation suitability.**
- **Protocol registration** and data availability.
- **Funding** and the supporter's role.

## Commonly skipped (writer checklist)
- The **experimental unit** and the true **n** (items 1, 2a) — cage or animal; frequently confused.
- **Randomization and blinding** (4, 5) — the most under-reported in in-vivo studies.
- The **ethical approval number** and **3R** — most journals require them.
- In the results, the **effect size + 95% CI** (item 10); the p-value alone is insufficient.
